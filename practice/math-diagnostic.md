|---|---|---|
| **26+** and no section with 2+ wrong | Small gaps | Follow [[study-plan]] as written. 30 min/day of drills on any section with a miss, weeks 1–2 only. |
| **20–25**, or 2+ wrong in **one** section | Patchy | Same as above, but drills run weeks 1–4 and target only the weak sections. Retake those sections at the end of week 2. |
