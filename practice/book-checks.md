# Book Checks

Places where the textbooks contradict themselves or contain a likely error. Don't memorize the wrong version.

If you find another one, add it here with the book and page.

| Chapter | What to watch for |
|---|---|
| [[Hist Ch4 The Middle Ages and Early Modern World]] | Grade 9 dates the fall of the Byzantine Empire to **1453**, but the Grade 11 summary says **1454**, even though Grade 11 uses 1453 elsewhere. Use 1453, the standard date. |
| [[Hist Ch11 Colonial Africa]] | The Grade 12 summary spells the British administrator "Luggard". The usual spelling is Lugard. |
| [[Hist Ch17 Ethiopia After 1991]] | Grade 12 contradicts itself on GERD's capacity: the body says **6000 Mega Watts**, but the summary says "6000 kilowatts". Use megawatts. |
| [[Geog Ch2 Geology and Landforms]] | The Grade 11 summary says the Phanerozoic Eon is divided into four eras including the Precambrian. The Precambrian comes before the Phanerozoic, so treat the four eras as a sequence, as Grade 9 does. |
| [[Geog Ch3 Climate]] | The Grade 9 summary lists the first climate controls as "latitude, latitude". The second means altitude, Ethiopia's most important control. |
| [[Geog Ch3 Climate]] | The Grade 11 summary mislabels Köppen's group E. Use E = polar. |
| [[Geog Ch8 Contemporary Geographic Issues]] | The Grade 12 summary says "Deforestation is a type of land degradation" in drylands. The definition describes **desertification**, so learn it that way. |
| [[Econ Ch13 The Ethiopian Economy]] | Figures for people working in agriculture differ: Grade 10 Economics says more than 85%, while Grade 9 Geography says more than 80%. Say "over 80%" unless a question gives its own figure. |
