# Hist Ch2 Ancient World Civilizations

Compare civilizations rather than memorizing them one at a time: what each was built on, how it was ruled, and what it left behind.

## Must know
- Egypt's civilization dates from the early 4th millennium BC. It is divided into Pre-dynastic, Old, Middle and New Kingdoms under pharaohs regarded as gods (9.2 §2.1).
- Nubia was known for building and especially for its iron industry, and it influenced and was influenced by Egypt (9.2 §2.1).
- Sumer, between the Euphrates and Tigris, was a set of warring city-states rather than one kingdom. Sumerians are credited with the plough, a writing system and the wheel (9.2 §2.2).
- Greek history here centres on the wars with Persia, the Peloponnesian War, and classical and Hellenistic culture (9.2 §2.4).
- Christianity spread especially after Constantine made it an official religion of the Roman Empire (9.2 §2.5).
- Lasting ancient legacies include irrigation, the 365-day calendar, monotheism and medicine (11.2 summary).
