# Hist Ch12 Ethiopia Mid-19th Century to 1941

Modern Ethiopia was made by two processes at once: unifying the north and expanding into the south. Its independence was defended against Egypt and Italy, which then returned in 1935.

## Must know
- Long-distance trade linked south and north from Bonga, run largely by Afkala and Jabarti traders, using amole salt bars and Maria Theresa Thalers (10.3 §3.1).
- Tewodros II (1855–1868) first attempted to unify the country (10.3 §3.2).
- Yohannes IV favoured decentralized rule. Menelik II expanded the territory, which created Ethiopia's present shape (10.3 §3.2, 12.3 §3.3).
- Napier's British expedition led to Tewodros's death (10.3 §3.3).
- Ethiopia defeated Egypt at Gundat (1875) and Gura (1876), and Italy at Adwa (1896) (10.3 §3.3, 12.3 §3.5).
- After Menelik, Taytu, Iyasu, Zewditu and Teferi competed for power. Teferi became Haile Selassie I in 1930 (12.3 §3.6).
- Italy invaded in 1935, patriots limited its rule to the main towns, and British intervention followed Italy's June 1940 war declaration (12.3 §3.7).

## Timeline
- 1855–1868: reign of Tewodros II
- 1875: Battle of Gundat
- 1876: Battle of Gura
- 1896: Battle of Adwa
- 1930: Teferi crowned Haile Selassie I
- 1935: Italian invasion
- June 1940: Italy declares war on Britain and France; British intervention follows
