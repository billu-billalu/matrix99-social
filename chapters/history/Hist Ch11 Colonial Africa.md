# Hist Ch11 Colonial Africa

Africa's colonial story has three parts: why Europeans came, how Africans resisted, and how colonial rule worked and changed Africa.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Hist 10.2 Africa and the Colonial Experience]]: study §2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 2.6; skim §2.5
- [ ] [[Hist 12.2 Africa and the Colonial Experience]]: study all

## Must know
- The Industrial Revolution increased the demand for African raw materials and markets (10.2 §2.2, 12.2 §2.1).
- The Scramble for Africa peaked in the late 1880s and 1890s, and the Berlin Conference of 1884–85 tried to regulate it (10.2 §2.3, 12.2 §2.1).
- Resistance included Samori Toure, Urabi Pasha, the Mahdist Movement and the Maji-Maji Rebellion (12.2 §2.2).
- Britain used Indirect Rule, formulated by Frederick Lugard, and France used Direct Rule, with Albert Sarraut as a key figure (12.2 §2.3).
- Colonialism weakened Africa economically, politically and culturally (10.2 §2.6).

## Timeline
- 1884–85: Berlin Conference
- Late 1880s–1890s: peak of the Scramble
