# Hist Ch14 Global Developments Since 1945

WWII left two superpowers. Their rivalry shaped Europe, Asia, the Middle East and even the UN's work, until the Soviet bloc collapsed.

## Must know
- WWII shifted the world from multi-polar to bipolar, dominated by the USA and USSR (12.5 §5.1).
- It also led to or shaped the UN, World Bank, IMF and WTO, plus nuclear fission, computers and jet engines (12.5 summary).
- The UN aims to keep peace and security, build friendly relations and encourage cooperation and human rights. Grade 10 is the main section (10.5 §5.1).
- The Marshall Plan (1948) sped Western Europe's recovery, while Eastern Europe became Soviet satellites (12.5 §5.3).
- Containment was the US response to communist expansion (10.5 §5.2).
- The Non-Aligned Movement is covered mainly in Grade 10 (10.5 §5.4).
- Gorbachev's reforms, opposition in the Eastern Bloc and the 1989 movements led to the USSR breaking apart (10.5 §5.6, 12.5 §5.7).

## Timeline
- June 26, 1945: UN established (date as given in Grade 12)
- 1948: Marshall Plan launched
- 1989: anti-communist movements spread across the Eastern Bloc
