# Hist Ch18 Indigenous Knowledge and Heritage

Indigenous knowledge is treated as a working resource for agriculture, medicine, conflict resolution and more. Heritage is what connects that knowledge to identity.

## Must know
- Indigenous knowledge is built up outside formal education over generations to fit a local environment (12.9 §9.1).
- It covers areas such as agriculture, architecture, medicine, cottage industry, philosophy and conflict resolution (12.9 §9.1–9.2).
- Heritage is everything people inherit and want to pass on, whether tangible or intangible (10.9 §9.2).
- Geez literature is mostly related to the Orthodox Church, but it includes many original texts. Muslim learning centres also hold important Arabic materials (12.9 Heritages).
- Dawuro's dry-stone defensive walls are called Kati Halala Keela (12.9 Heritages).
- Festivals to know include Ashenda, Irreecha and Fichee-Chambalaalla (12.9 Heritages).
