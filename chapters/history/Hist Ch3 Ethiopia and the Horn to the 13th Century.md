# Hist Ch3 Ethiopia and the Horn to the 13th Century

This is the foundation of Ethiopian history: who lived in the region, what they believed, and how states from Punt to Zagwe rose through trade and agriculture.

## Must know
- Ethiopia's more than 85 languages are grouped under Cushitic, Semitic, Omotic and Nilo-Saharan families. Grade 9 groups the first three under Afro-Asiatic (9.3 §3.1, 11.3 §3.1).
- Major religions: indigenous religions, Judaism (Bete-Israel), Christianity and Islam (9.3 §3.1).
- Christianity reached Ethiopia in the early 4th century AD. Catholicism came through the royal court from the later 16th century, and Protestantism spread mainly in the 20th (9.3 §3.1).
- Islam was founded around 610 AD. Early Muslim refugees came to Ethiopia, and Islam reached the interior through the port of Zeila (9.3 §3.1).
- Aksum was not the first polity: Punt, Damat and other states came before it (9.3 §3.2, 11.3 §3.2).
- Aksum's income came from agriculture and later mainly from Red Sea trade (9.3 §3.3).
- The Sultanate of Shewa was the earliest of the Muslim sultanates (9.3 §3.5).

## Timeline
- Early 4th century AD: Christianity introduced to Ethiopia
- About 610 AD: Islam founded; early Muslim refugees later arrive in Ethiopia
