# Hist Ch8 Ethiopia Mid-16th to Mid-19th Century

Power was decentralized in this period: independent states flourished in the south, west and east, while the Christian kingdom moved to Gondar and then fragmented.

## Must know
- From the mid-16th to mid-19th century political power was decentralized, with independent states across the region (9.8 §8.1).
- Peoples of the south, west and east had long traditions of state formation (11.8 §8.1).
- Jesuit efforts to make Ethiopia Catholic brought violence and long doctrinal controversy (11.8 §8.2).
- Gondar became the capital in 1636 and grew through building, trade and cash payment for services (9.8 §8.2).
- The Zemene Mesafint began around 1769, weakening central authority and strengthening regional lords (11.8 §8.2.4).
- Ali founded the Yejju dynasty in 1786, which controlled northern politics through puppet monarchs (9.8 §8.4).
- The Shewan kingdom grew in Manz under its founder Negasi (9.8 §8.5).

## Timeline
- 1636: Gondar becomes the capital
- About 1769: Zemene Mesafint begins
- 1786: Yejju dynasty established by Ali
- Mid-19th century: Zemene Mesafint ends
