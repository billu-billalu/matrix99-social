# Hist Ch9 The Age of Revolutions

Enlightenment ideas and economic change produced revolutions that destroyed feudal orders and spread liberty, equality and nationalism. Study causes and results side by side.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Hist 9.9 The Age of Revolutions 1750s-1815]]: study §9.3, 9.5; skim §9.1, 9.2, 9.4
- [ ] [[Hist 11.9 The Age of Revolutions 1789-1815]]: study all

## Must know
- The Industrial Revolution helped establish a fully capitalist system (9.9 §9.1, 11.9 §9.1).
- The English Revolution of the 17th century was a bourgeois revolution that cleared the way for capitalism (9.9 summary).
- The American War freed 13 colonies and helped cause the French Revolution (9.9 §9.4, 11.9 §9.2).
- The Enlightenment had created a revolutionary situation in France before 1789 (9.9 summary, 11.4 §4.8).
- The French Revolution of 1789 destroyed the old feudal system and spread ideas of liberty and equality (11.9 §9.3).
- Napoleon spread these ideas through conquest, but his rule awakened the nationalism that helped bring him down (9.9 §9.3).
- Metternich played a dominant role at the Congress of Vienna (9.9 §9.5, 11.9 objectives).

## Timeline
- 17th century: English Revolution
- 1789: French Revolution begins
- 1815: Congress of Vienna settlement closes the Napoleonic period
