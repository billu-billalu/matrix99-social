# Hist Ch10 Capitalism and Nationalism

Capitalism and nationalism were the two great forces of the 19th century. Together they redrew Europe and fed the imperialism of Chapter 11.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Hist 10.1 Development of Capitalism and Nationalism 1815-1914]]: skim all
- [ ] [[Hist 12.1 Development of Capitalism and Nationalism 1815-1914]]: study all

## Must know
- Capitalism is defined by private ownership of the means of production, and it divided society into capitalist and working classes (12.1 §1.1).
- Industrial capitalism was first established in Britain (c.1750/1780–1851) (12.1 §1.2).
- Nationalism grew from shared identity, especially custom, language and religion (12.1 §1.3).
- Italy used the 1866 Austro-Prussian War and the 1870–71 Franco-Prussian War to complete unification (12.1 §1.4).
- Otto von Bismarck was the architect of German unification, from a 39-state confederation led by Austria (12.1 §1.5).
- The American Civil War (1861–1865) grew from the dispute over slavery and ended with its abolition (10.1 §1.2.3).
- The Eastern Question and Balkan crises unsettled the whole of Europe (12.1 §1.7).

## Timeline
- c.1750/1780–1851: Industrial Revolution in Britain
- 1861–1865: American Civil War
- 1866: Austro-Prussian War
- 1870–71: Franco-Prussian War
