# Hist Ch5 Peoples and States of Africa to 1500

Learn this unit by region. It also counters the colonial claim that Africa had no history, which the Grade 11 book directly rejects.

- [ ] [[Hist 9.5 Peoples and States of Africa to 1500]]: skim all

## Must know
- Africa's four language families are Niger-Congo, Afro-Asiatic, Nilo-Saharan and Khoisan (9.5 §5.1).
- Mamluks ruled Egypt from 1250 to 1517 and often clashed with Ethiopia (9.5 §5.2).
- Controlling Trans-Saharan trade helped Ghana, Mali, Songhay, the Hausa States and Kanem-Bornu grow (9.5 §5.4, §5.8).
- Islam's spread strengthened links between tropical and North Africa (9.5 §5.3, §5.8).
- Great Zimbabwe reached a high level of civilization. After about 1450, Mwene Mutapa arose among the Shona (9.5 §5.6).
- The Swahili of the East Coast developed advanced maritime culture (11.5 summary).

## Timeline
- 1250–1517: Mamluk rule in Egypt
- After 1450: Mwene Mutapa empire established
