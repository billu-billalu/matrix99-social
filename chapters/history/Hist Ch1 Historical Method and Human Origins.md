# Hist Ch1 Historical Method and Human Origins

History rests on evidence, so you need to know how historians use sources before you judge any claim. The second half traces humans from the earliest tool-makers to the first states.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Hist 9.1 The Discipline of History and Human Evolution]]: skim all
- [ ] [[Hist 11.1 History Historiography and Human Evolution]]: study all

## Must know
- Prehistory is the long period before writing; history covers the human past after writing began (9.1 §1.1).
- Sources divide into primary and secondary. Historiography studies how knowledge of the past is gained and passed on (9.1 §1.2, 11.1 §1.1).
- History became a separate professional field in Europe at the start of the 19th century (11.1 §1.1).
- Writing on imperial Ethiopia, up to 1974, suffered from unbalanced sources and weak objectivity (11.1 §1.1).
- The two opposing explanations of human origins are creationist and evolutionist (9.1 §1.3).
- The Stone Age ran from about 2.5 million to 8000 B.P., and people lived by hunting and gathering (11.1 §1.2).
- The Neolithic Revolution, the domestication of plants and animals, made settled life possible (11.1 §1.2).
