# Hist Ch15 Ethiopia 1941-1991

Fifty years of Ethiopian history in one chapter: autocracy under Haile Selassie, revolution in 1974, and the Derg's fall in 1991. Land, famine and war connect all of it.

- [ ] [[Hist 10.6 Ethiopia Internal Developments and External Influences 1941-1991]]: skim all
- [ ] [[Hist 12.6 Ethiopia Internal Developments and External Influences 1941-1991]]: study all

## Must know
- Britain dominated Ethiopia after liberation. Eritrea was federated with Ethiopia in 1952, and the entire Ogaden was restored in 1954 (12.6 §6.1).
- Agriculture was over 60% of GDP and 80% of the labour force. Rist prevailed in the north and gult in the south (12.6 §6.2).
- Opposition took the form of plots, coups, peasant rebellions and student movements (12.6 §6.3).
- About 120 junior officers formed the Derg on June 28, 1974 (12.6 §6.4).
- The 1975 land reform nationalized rural land, and the Derg adopted Marxism-Leninism (10.6 §6.3, 12.6 §6.5).
- The 1984/5 famine and failed resettlement and villagization deepened the crisis (10.6 summary).
- Armed fronts, lost Soviet support and civil war brought the EPRDF into Addis Ababa on May 28, 1991 (10.6 §6.4).

## Timeline
- 1952: Eritrea federated with Ethiopia
- 1954: Ogaden restored
- 1960: abortive coup
- February 1974: revolution erupts
- June 28, 1974: Derg established
- 1975: land reform
- 1977: Ethio-Somalia War
- 1984/5: famine
- May 28, 1991: EPRDF takes Addis Ababa
