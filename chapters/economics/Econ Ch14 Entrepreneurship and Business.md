# Econ Ch14 Entrepreneurship and Business

Practical and self-contained. Learn the definitions, then practise applying them to a real business idea.

## Reading path

## Must know
- An entrepreneur creates a new business, bearing risks and enjoying rewards (9.8 §8.1).
- A social entrepreneur seeks business opportunities that benefit the community (9.8 §8.1).
- Creativity means new ideas, and innovation means putting them to use to create value (9.8 §8.2, 10.8 §8.1).
- Startups may not make profit for years because they aim to grow quickly (10.8 §8.2).
- Business forms are sole ownership, partnership and corporation (10.8 §8.3).
- Cost-benefit analysis weighs an action's benefits against its costs (10.8 §8.4).
