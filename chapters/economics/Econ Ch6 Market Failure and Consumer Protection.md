# Econ Ch6 Market Failure and Consumer Protection

Markets do not always allocate resources well. This chapter explains why, and it justifies many government roles in Chapter 10.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 12.3 Market Failure and Consumer Protection]]: study all

## Must know
- Market failure is an inefficient distribution of goods in a free market. Causes include externalities, monopoly and information asymmetry (12.3 §3.1).
- Public goods are non-excludable and non-rivalrous, which leads to free riders (12.3 §3.2).
- An externality is a cost or benefit to an uninvolved party (12.3 §3.3).
- Asymmetric information arises when some parties know more than others (12.3 §3.4).
- Consumer protection guards consumers against unethical business practices (12.3 §3.5).
