# Econ Ch7 Money Banking and Finance

Learn what money does, then how Ethiopian institutions, both formal and indigenous, move it between savers and borrowers.

## Must know
- Money is anything generally accepted as payment. It replaced barter (9.6 §6.1–6.2).
- Functions: medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value and standard of deferred payment (9.6 §6.3).
- In Ethiopia, amole salt bars and Maria Theresa thalers served as money (9.6 §6.2).
- People hold money for transaction, precautionary and speculative motives (9.6 §6.4).
- Modern banking began in Ethiopia in 1905 with the Bank of Abyssinia (10.5 §5.4).
- Microfinance serves people the traditional banking system leaves out (10.5 §5.5).
- Iqqub and iddir are indigenous financial institutions (10.5 §5.7).
