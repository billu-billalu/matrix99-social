# Econ Ch9 Aggregate Analysis

What households spend and firms invest together drive aggregate demand. That leads directly to the AD-AS model of the whole economy.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 11.4 Consumption Saving and Investment]]: study all
- [ ] [[Econ 12.2 Aggregate Demand and Aggregate Supply Analysis]]: study all

## Must know
- Disposable income determines household consumption (11.4 §4.1).
- APC + APS = 1 and MPC + MPS = 1 (11.4 §4.3).
- Investment varies inversely with the real interest rate (11.4 §4.4).
- The accelerator theory links investment to demand for goods (11.4 §4.4).
- The AD curve slopes downward (12.2 §2.1).
- Short-run AS slopes upward, and long-run AS is vertical (12.2 §2.2).
- AD and AS together determine output and the price level (12.2 §2.3).

## Formulas
- APC = C ÷ Yd; MPC = change in C ÷ change in Yd (11.4 §4.1)
- APS = S ÷ Yd; MPS = change in S ÷ change in Yd (11.4 §4.2)
- APC + APS = 1; MPC + MPS = 1 (11.4 §4.3)
- Real interest rate = nominal interest rate − inflation (11.4 §4.4)
- AD = C + I + G + (X − M) (12.2 §2.1)
