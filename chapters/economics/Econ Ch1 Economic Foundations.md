# Econ Ch1 Economic Foundations

Economics starts from one problem: wants are unlimited and resources are scarce. Nearly everything later in the subject builds on that.

## Must know
- Economics studies the efficient allocation of scarce resources (9.1 §1.1).
- Microeconomics studies individual units, and macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole (9.1 §1.2).
- Deductive reasoning goes from principle to fact, and inductive goes from particular to general. Positive economics is factual, and normative involves value judgments (9.1 §1.3).
- The three decision-making units are households, firms and government (9.1 §1.4).
- The production possibility curve shows scarcity, choice and opportunity cost. Increasing opportunity cost makes it concave to the origin (9.2 §2.1).
- The four economic systems are traditional, capitalist, command and mixed (9.2 §2.3).
- Economic resources are land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship (9.3 §3.1).
- The circular flow model shows how households, firms and government interact in markets (9.3 §3.4).
