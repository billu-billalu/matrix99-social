# Econ Ch12 Development Poverty and Environment

Growth is not the same as development. This chapter asks what progress really means, who is left behind, and whether it can last.

## Must know
- Growth is one-dimensional, while development includes structural, social and institutional change (11.6 §6.1).
- HDI ranks countries using income and education and has been criticized as too narrow (11.6 §6.3).
- The eight MDGs had a 2015 target, and the SDGs run from 2015 to 2030 (11.6 §6.6–6.7).
- Poverty can be absolute or relative, and is defined through income, consumption or capability (12.6 §6.1).
- Inequality is shown by the Lorenz curve and Gini coefficient (12.6 §6.2).
- Poverty has global, regional and gender dimensions (12.6 §6.3–6.4).
- Growth that ignores social and environmental effects has global costs (12.8 §8.1).
- Ethiopia's Climate Resilient Green Economy strategy aims for carbon-neutral growth (12.8 §8.4).
