# Econ Ch2 Demand and Supply

Demand and supply is the most-used model in the subject. Master the difference between a movement along a curve and a shift, then elasticity.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 9.4 Introduction to Demand and Supply]]: skim all
- [ ] [[Econ 10.2 Theories of Demand and Supply]]: study all

## Must know
- Law of demand: with other things constant, quantity demanded rises as price falls (9.4 §4.1).
- Demand shifts with income, prices of substitutes and complements, tastes, expectations and number of consumers (10.2 §2.1).
- Market demand is the horizontal sum of individual demand (10.2 §2.1).
- Law of supply: quantity supplied rises with price, ceteris paribus (10.2 §2.2).
- Equilibrium is where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied, and it can be found graphically or mathematically (10.2 §2.3).
- Elasticity measures responsiveness to price, income or the price of another good (10.2 §2.4).

## Formulas
- Price elasticity of demand = % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price (10.2 §2.4)
- At equilibrium: Qd = Qs (10.2 §2.3)
