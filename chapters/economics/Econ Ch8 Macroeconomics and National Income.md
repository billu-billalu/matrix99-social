## Must know
- GDP is the market value of all final goods and services produced in a country in a year. GNP is based on a country's resources (11.3 §3.2).
- Three measurement approaches: expenditure, income and value-added (11.3 §3.3).
- Nominal GDP uses current prices, and real GDP uses constant prices (11.3 §3.5).
- Macro goals: growth, stable prices, low unemployment and a healthy external balance (9.7 §7.2, 10.6 §6.1).
- GDP misses leisure, non-market activity, environmental quality, income distribution and black-market trade (10.6 §6.4).
- The business cycle is irregular and hard to predict (10.6 §6.5).
- Schools of thought: classical and neoclassical, Keynesian, monetarist, new classical and new Keynesian. The Great Depression challenged the classical view (12.1 §1.3).

## Formulas
- GDP = C + I + G + NX (10.7 §7.1, 11.3 §3.3)
- GDP deflator = nominal GDP ÷ real GDP × 100 (11.3 §3.5)
- Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ labour force × 100 (9.7 §7.3)
