# Econ Ch11 International Trade and Finance

Why do countries trade, and how do they record and manage it? Comparative advantage is the most important idea here. Practise it with numbers.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 11.5 Trade and Finance]]: study all

## Must know
- Trade is domestic (retail and wholesale) or international (11.5 §5.1).
- Mercantilists tied wealth to precious metals, encouraging exports and discouraging imports (11.5 §5.3).
- Adam Smith's absolute advantage needs a cost advantage in at least one good (11.5 §5.3).
- Ricardo's comparative advantage shows that trade can benefit both countries even if one has an advantage in both goods (11.5 §5.3).
- The balance of payments records all transactions with the rest of the world, mainly in current and capital accounts (11.5 §5.4).
- Learn trade policies, exchange rate determination and regional integration (11.5 §5.5–5.7).
