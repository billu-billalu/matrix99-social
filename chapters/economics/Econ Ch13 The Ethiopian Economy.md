# Econ Ch13 The Ethiopian Economy

Compare Ethiopian economic policy across the Imperial, Derg and post-1991 periods. History explains why each government chose its path.

## Must know
- Agriculture is the mainstay of the Ethiopian economy (10.7 §7.3).
- Problems in agriculture are both human-made and natural (10.7 §7.3, 11.7 §7.3).
- Imperial policy favoured industry and commercial farmers over the wider agricultural sector (11.7 §7.1).
- Under the Derg, collective and state farms were favoured over individual farmers (11.7 §7.2).
- After 1991: ADLI, SDPRP, PASDEP, GTP I and II, and the ten-year plan (11.7 §7.1, 12.7 §7.1).
- The Monarchy ran Five-Year Plans for 1957–61, 1962–67 and 1968–73 (12.7 §7.1).
- Rapid growth under the GTPs still left challenges in sustaining it and sharing it fairly (12.7 §7.1).

## Timeline
- 1957–61, 1962–67, 1968–73: Imperial Five-Year Plans
- 1974–1991: Derg socialist planning
- 2002/03–2004/05: SDPRP
- 2005/06–2009/10: PASDEP
- 2010/11–2014/15: GTP I, followed by GTP II
