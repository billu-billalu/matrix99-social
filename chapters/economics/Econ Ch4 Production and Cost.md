# Econ Ch4 Production and Cost

This chapter asks how output and costs behave as a firm produces more. Expect tables to complete and curves to draw.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 9.5 Introduction to Production and Cost]]: skim all
- [ ] [[Econ 10.3 Theories of Production and Cost]]: study all

## Must know
- The short run has at least one fixed input, and in the long run all inputs vary (9.5 §5.2).
- TP is total output, AP is output per unit of variable input, and MP is the change in TP from one more unit (10.3 §3.1).
- Law of diminishing returns: adding one input while others are fixed eventually lowers extra output (10.3 §3.1).
- Explicit costs are payments to outsiders, and implicit costs are for inputs a firm owns (9.5 §5.3).
- Fixed costs exist even at zero output, and variable costs change with output (10.3 §3.2).
- Learn the stages of production, returns to scale and how production and cost curves relate (10.3 §3.1–3.2).

## Formulas
- AP = TP ÷ L; MP = change in TP ÷ change in L (10.3 §3.1)
- TC = TFC + TVC (10.3 §3.2)
- AC = TC ÷ Q; MC = change in TC ÷ change in Q (10.3 §3.2)
