# Econ Ch10 Macroeconomic Policy and Tax

Government can steer the economy through spending and tax, money, incomes and exchange rates. Know each tool and which direction it pushes.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Econ 12.4 Macroeconomic Policy Instruments]]: study all

## Must know
- Expansionary policy answers deficient demand, and contractionary policy answers excess demand (12.4 §4.1).
- Expansionary fiscal policy means more spending or lower tax rates, and contractionary means the reverse (12.4 §4.2).
- Expansionary monetary policy means a lower bank rate, buying securities and a lower reserve ratio, and contractionary means the reverse (12.4 §4.3).
- Taxes are compulsory, unrequited payments to government (12.5 §5.1).
- Taxes raise revenue, redistribute income and discourage harmful goods (12.5 §5.1).
- Direct taxes fall on the person earning the income, and the burden of indirect taxes passes to someone else (12.5 §5.1).
- Taxes are progressive, proportional or regressive. Equity is horizontal or vertical (12.5 §5.1–5.2).
- In Ethiopia taxes are classified by Schedules, and avoidance and evasion remain a challenge (12.5 §5.4–5.5).
