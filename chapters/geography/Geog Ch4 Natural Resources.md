## Must know
- Rivers flow from the central highlands to the lowlands, and many cross borders. Ethiopia is called the "Water Tower of East Africa" (9.3 §3.2).
- Nithosols (also spelled nitosols) are the most widely used soil type, supporting cereals, coffee and enset (9.3 §3.4).
- Vegetation follows altitude and rainfall, and there are five major types (9.3 §3.6).
- The Congo is the second-largest river by volume, and the Nile is the longest (10.3 §3.2).
- Sustainable use means meeting present needs without long-term degradation (10.3 summary).
- There are more than 263 transboundary river and lake basins, covering 145 nations (11.3 §3.4).
- Unfair use of shared water can lead to regional conflict. Compare Ethiopia, Sudan and Egypt (11.3 §3.6–3.7).
- Resources are renewable (flow) or non-renewable (fund). Poor management can drive conflict (12.3 §3.2).
- Indigenous conflict resolution is a recognized part of managing resources (12.3 §3.4).
