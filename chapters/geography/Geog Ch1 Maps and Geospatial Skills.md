## Must know
- A map is a simplified, reduced representation of the earth's surface, used for location, distance, area and direction (9.8 §8.1).
- Scale can be shown three ways: statement, representative fraction and graphic scale (9.8 §8.2).
- Direction is measured clockwise from north, in compass points or degree bearings (9.8 §8.3).
- Latitude and longitude form the most accurate international way to locate places (9.8 §8.3).
- Spatial data come from ground survey, GPS and remote sensing (10.8 §8.2).
- Digital data are stored as vector (points, lines, polygons) or raster (10.8 §8.3).
- On contour maps, concentric closed contours show a hill, V-shapes pointing uphill show valleys, and tick marks show depressions (11.8 §8.1).
- Geographic research is quantitative, qualitative or mixed (12.8 §8.1).

## Formulas
- Representative fraction = map distance ÷ ground distance, in the same units (9.8 §8.2)
