## Must know
- Ethiopian resource degradation stems from population pressure, agricultural expansion, migration, urbanization, resettlement, climate change and pollution (9.7 §7.1).
- Soil erosion by running water is the major soil problem. Responses include terracing, check dams and afforestation (9.7 §7.1).
- Forest cover fell from 40% to under 3%. Grade 9 reports about 15.7% under the Green Legacy strategy (9.7 §7.2).
- Unplanned urbanization risks instability, infrastructure strain, water crises and disease (10.7 §7.1).
- Malthusians see population and economic growth as inversely related, whereas Marx and Boserup see it as positive (11.7 §7.1).
- Desertification is land degradation in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas, from both people and climate (12.7 §7.2).
- Drought types: meteorological, hydrological and agricultural (12.7 §7.3).
- Eastern Africa's drylands, about 65% of the region, are highly vulnerable (12.7 summary).
