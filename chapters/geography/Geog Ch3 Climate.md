## Must know
- Weather is short-term, and climate averages weather over a long period, 30 years or more (10.2 summary).
- Climate elements include temperature, precipitation, humidity, pressure and wind. Controls determine how they vary (9.2 §2.2–2.3).
- Ethiopia's agro-ecological zones range from Bereha to Wurch, and its four seasons are Kiremt, Meher, Bega and Belg (9.2 §2.4).
- Ethiopia has five main rainfall regions (9.2 §2.4).
- Köppen's five main groups: A tropical, B arid, C mid-latitude mild, D mid-latitude cold, E polar. The system rests on vegetation, temperature and precipitation (11.2 §2.2).
- Köppen's weaknesses include ignoring factors such as cloudiness, rainy days and winds (11.2 summary).
- Climate change has natural causes, including Milankovitch cycles, solar output and volcanoes, and human causes, especially greenhouse gases (12.2 §2.3).
- Global average temperatures have risen by about 1°C since the late 19th century (12.2 §2.2).
- Ethiopia's response is the Climate Resilient Green Economy strategy (12.2 §2.7).
