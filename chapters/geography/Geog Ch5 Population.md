## Must know
- Ethiopia's population figures in Grade 9: 114.9 million, growing 2.6% a year, second largest in Africa. Check for newer figures, since these date with the book (9.4 §4.2).
- Fertility, mortality and migration are the three components of population change (10.4 §4.2).
- About 80% of Ethiopians live in rural areas (9.4 §4.5).
- Africa's urbanization rate is the highest in the world (10.4 §4.5).
- LDCs face rapid growth, while MDCs face ageing. The demographic dividend requires investment in education and health (11.4 §4.1).
- Malthus proposed preventive and positive checks. Neo-Malthusians add birth control (12.4 §4.1).
- Boserup argued population growth stimulates agricultural innovation. Marx blamed poverty on lack of access to the means of subsistence (12.4 §4.1).
- Population policies are pro-natalist or anti-natalist (12.4 §4.2).

## Formulas
- Age dependency ratio: dependent-age population ÷ working-age population × 100 (9.4 §4.3)
- Population density = population ÷ area (9.4 §4.4)
