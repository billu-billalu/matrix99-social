# Math Ch8 Matrices and Determinants

Matrices organize systems of equations. Determinants tell you whether a unique solution exists and help find it.

## Must know
- Matrices multiply only if columns of the first equal rows of the second, and generally AB ≠ BA (11.3 §3.2).
- Row operations reach echelon form and solve systems by Gaussian elimination (11.3 §3.4, §3.6).
- A system can have one solution, none or infinitely many (11.3 §3.6).
- A square matrix has an inverse only if its determinant is not 0 (11.4 §4.5).
- Know the determinant's properties, such as a row swap changing its sign (11.4 §4.4).
- Cramer's rule solves systems using determinants (11.4 §4.6).

## Formulas
- 2×2 determinant: ad − bc (11.4 §4.1)
- 2×2 inverse: (1/(ad − bc)) × [d, −b; −c, a] (11.4 §4.5)
- Cramer's rule: x = Dₓ/D, y = D_y/D (11.4 §4.6)
