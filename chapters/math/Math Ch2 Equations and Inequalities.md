# Math Ch2 Equations and Inequalities

Solving equations and inequalities is the core skill of all later Math. Practise until the methods are automatic.

## Must know
- Solve systems of two linear equations by substitution or elimination (9.3 §3.2).
- Solve quadratics by factoring, completing the square or the formula (9.3 §3.3).
- The discriminant b² − 4ac shows whether there are two, one or no real roots (9.3 §3.3).
- Multiplying or dividing an inequality by a negative reverses its sign (9.4 §4.1).
- |x| < a means −a < x < a, and |x| > a means x < −a or x > a (9.4 §4.3).
- Use a sign chart for quadratic inequalities (9.4 §4.4).

## Formulas
- x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a (9.3 §3.3)
- Sum of roots = −b/a; product of roots = c/a (9.3 §3.3)
