# Math Ch6 Coordinate Geometry and Transformations

Coordinate geometry turns shapes into algebra. Transformations then move those shapes in predictable ways.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Math 10.7 Coordinate Geometry]] §7.1–7.4: distance, division, lines, slopes
- [ ] §7.5: circle equation
- [ ] [[Math 11.6 Transformations of the Plane]] §6.1–6.5

## Must know
- Parallel lines have equal slopes (10.7 §7.4).
- Perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is −1 (10.7 §7.4).
- Complete the square to find a circle's centre and radius (10.7 §7.5).
- Translations, reflections and rotations are rigid motions, preserving size and shape (11.6 §6.1).

## Formulas
- Distance = √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²) (10.7 §7.1)
- Midpoint = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2) (10.7 §7.2)
- Slope m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); y = mx + c; y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) (10.7 §7.3)
- Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r² (10.7 §7.5)
- Reflection in y = x: (x, y) → (y, x) (11.6 §6.3)
- Rotation 90° anticlockwise about the origin: (x, y) → (−y, x) (11.6 §6.4)
