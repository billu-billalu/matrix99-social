# Math Ch10 Sequences and Series

Sequences add a constant or multiply by a constant. Once you can tell which, the formulas follow, and they connect directly to growth and interest.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Math 12.1 Sequences and Series]] §1.1–1.3
- [ ] §1.4–1.5: infinite series, applications

## Must know
- A sequence can be defined by an explicit or recursive rule (12.1 §1.1).
- Arithmetic sequences add a common difference, and geometric sequences multiply by a common ratio (12.1 §1.2).
- Sigma notation is a short way to write sums (12.1 §1.3).
- An infinite geometric series converges only when |r| < 1 (12.1 §1.4).

## Formulas
- Arithmetic: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d; Sₙ = (n/2)(2a + (n − 1)d) (12.1 §1.2–1.3)
- Geometric: aₙ = ar^(n−1); Sₙ = a(1 − rⁿ)/(1 − r) (12.1 §1.2–1.3)
- Infinite geometric: S = a/(1 − r), |r| < 1 (12.1 §1.4)
