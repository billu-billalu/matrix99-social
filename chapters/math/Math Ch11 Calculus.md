# Math Ch11 Calculus

Calculus studies change. The derivative gives a rate or slope, and the integral adds up to an area. Learn the rules, then practise applications.

## Reading path
- [ ] [[Math 12.2 Introduction to Calculus]] §2.1: limits, derivatives, rules
- [ ] §2.2: applications (tangent, max/min, rates)
- [ ] §2.3: integration, area

## Must know
- A derivative is the limit of the average rate of change (12.2 §2.1).
- The derivative gives the slope of the tangent at a point (12.2 §2.2).
- A function increases where f′(x) > 0 and decreases where f′(x) < 0 (12.2 §2.2).
- Maxima and minima occur at critical points, where f′(x) = 0 or is undefined (12.2 §2.2).
- Integration reverses differentiation, and definite integrals can give areas (12.2 §2.3).

## Formulas
- d/dx(xⁿ) = nxⁿ⁻¹ (12.2 §2.1)
- ∫xⁿ dx = xⁿ⁺¹/(n + 1) + C, n ≠ −1 (12.2 §2.3)
- Definite integral from a to b of f(x) dx = F(b) − F(a) (12.2 §2.3)
