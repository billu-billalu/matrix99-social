# Math Ch7 Vectors

Vectors carry both size and direction. Grade 11 adds 3-D and the two products, which appear in Physics as well.

## Must know
- A scalar has size only, and a vector has size and direction (9.8 §8.1).
- Add vectors component by component, and scalar multiples stretch them (9.8 §8.3).
- A position vector locates a point relative to the origin (9.8 §8.4).
- Two non-zero vectors are perpendicular when their dot product is 0 (11.5 §5.3).
- The cross product gives a vector perpendicular to both originals (11.5 §5.3).

## Formulas
- |a| = √(x² + y²) in 2-D; √(x² + y² + z²) in 3-D (11.5 §5.2)
- a · b = x₁x₂ + y₁y₂ + z₁z₂ = |a||b| cos θ (11.5 §5.3)
