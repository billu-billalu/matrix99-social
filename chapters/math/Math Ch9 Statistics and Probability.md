# Math Ch9 Statistics and Probability

Statistics summarizes data, and probability measures uncertainty. Learn to count outcomes correctly, since many probability errors begin there.

## Must know
- Data can be presented in tables, histograms, frequency polygons and cumulative frequency curves (11.7 §7.3).
- Mean, median and mode describe the centre of data (9.9 §9.1, 11.7 §7.4).
- Range, variance and standard deviation describe spread (12.3 §3.1).
- The coefficient of variation compares spread between data sets (12.3 §3.2).
- Sampling methods include random, systematic, stratified and cluster (12.3 §3.4).
- Use permutations when order matters and combinations when it doesn't (11.8 §8.3).
- Mutually exclusive events cannot occur together, while independent events don't affect each other (11.8 §8.6).

## Formulas
- nPr = n!/(n − r)!; nCr = n!/(r!(n − r)!) (11.8 §8.3)
- P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B) (11.8 §8.7)
- Independent events: P(A∩B) = P(A) × P(B) (11.8 §8.7)
- Coefficient of variation = (standard deviation ÷ mean) × 100 (12.3 §3.2)
