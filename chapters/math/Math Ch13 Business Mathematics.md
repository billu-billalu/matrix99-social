# Math Ch13 Business Mathematics

This is Math you will use in real life: percentages, interest, loans and tax. It also connects directly to Economics.

- [ ] §5.3–5.4: loans, taxation

## Must know
- Work with ratio, proportion, percentage and profit or loss (12.5 §5.1).
- Simple interest is on the principal only, while compound interest is also on earlier interest (12.5 §5.2).
- Time value of money compares money across time using present and future value (12.5 §5.2).
- Know saving, investing, borrowing and loan repayment (12.5 §5.3).
- Know how to calculate Ethiopian income tax and VAT (12.5 §5.4).

## Formulas
- Simple interest: I = Prt (12.5 §5.2)
- Compound amount: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) (12.5 §5.2)
- Profit % = (profit ÷ cost) × 100 (12.5 §5.1)
