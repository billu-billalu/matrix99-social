## Must know
- Congruent triangles are shown by SSS, SAS, ASA or RHS (9.7 §7.1).
- Similar triangles are shown by AA, SAS or SSS (9.7 §7.3).
- If the side ratio is k, perimeters are in ratio k and areas are in ratio k² (9.7 §7.4–7.5).
- An inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc (10.5 §5.2).
- Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add to 180° (10.5 §5.2).
- Volume problems often combine solids, so split them into parts (10.6 §6.4).

## Formulas
- Sum of interior angles = (n − 2) × 180°; sum of exterior angles = 360° (9.6 §6.1–6.2)
- Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr; sector area = (θ/360) × πr² (10.5 §5.3)
- Cylinder V = πr²h; cone V = (1/3)πr²h; sphere V = (4/3)πr³, A = 4πr² (10.6 §6.1–6.2)
- Pyramid V = (1/3) × base area × h (10.6 §6.2)
