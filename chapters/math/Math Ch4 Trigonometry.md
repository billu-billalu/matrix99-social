## Must know
- Sine, cosine and tangent are ratios of sides in a right-angled triangle (9.5 §5.2).
- Memorize values at 30°, 45° and 60° (9.5 §5.2).
- Convert degrees to radians using 180° = π radians (10.4 §4.1).
- Signs of trigonometric functions depend on the quadrant, and reference angles give the values (10.4 §4.2).
- Trigonometry solves heights and distances (10.4 §4.4).

## Formulas
- sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse; cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse; tan θ = opposite/adjacent (9.5 §5.2)
- a² + b² = c² (9.5 §5.1)
- sin²θ + cos²θ = 1; tan θ = sin θ/cos θ (10.4 §4.3)
