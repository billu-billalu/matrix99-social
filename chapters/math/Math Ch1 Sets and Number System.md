# Math Ch1 Sets and Number System

Sets give you the language of Math, and the number system gives you the rules for calculating exactly. Weakness here causes errors in every later chapter.

- [ ] [[Math 9.1 Further on Sets]] §1.1–1.5

- [ ] §2.4–2.5: real-number properties, exponents, radicals

## Must know
- Sets can be described by listing, in words or with set-builder notation (9.1 §1.2).
- Know union, intersection, complement and difference, and show them on Venn diagrams (9.1 §1.4).
- A rational number can be written as p/q with q ≠ 0, and repeating decimals are rational (9.2 §2.2).
- Irrational numbers, such as √2, have non-repeating, non-terminating decimals (9.2 §2.3).
- Rationalize a denominator by multiplying top and bottom by a suitable surd (9.2 §2.3).
- Real numbers include rational and irrational numbers (9.2 §2.4).

## Formulas
- n(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B) (9.1 §1.5)
- GCF(a, b) × LCM(a, b) = a × b (9.2 §2.1)
- a^m × a^n = a^(m+n); (a^m)^n = a^(mn); a^(−n) = 1/a^n (9.2 §2.4)
