# Study Plan to July 2027

Pick your start date and adjust the weeks to your school calendar. Keep the **pairings**: chapters in the same week share a bridge, so each one makes the others easier. History runs chronologically, Economics and Math follow prerequisites, and Geography is placed to match them.

## Before you start
Take [[math-diagnostic]] once. Its result tells you how much extra Math time to plan for in weeks 1-4.

Weeks marked **(catch-up)** have no new Math, so use them to finish an unfinished Math chapter. Aim for about 80% on its unit review exercises, closed-book, then move on.

## Phase 2: Weighted second pass and past papers (about 8 weeks)
- Map past-paper questions to units in `practice/` and rank chapters by frequency.
- Re-study weak areas and top-weighted chapters using your "Make" sheets, not full re-reading.
- One timed past paper per subject per week; every miss goes in [[error-log]].

## Phase 3: Exam simulation (about 8 weeks)
- Full mock exams under real timing, 2–3 per week.
- Daily error-log review; re-test every logged mistake after 1, 3 and 7 days.

## Phase 4: Taper (final 1–2 weeks)
- "Make" sheets, bridge notes and the error log only. Sleep.

## Daily loop
1. Open the chapter guide and read **Must know**, so you know what to look for.
2. Tick the next reading-path item. Study CORE closely, and read SKIM quickly.
3. Close the book and write what you remember from that section.
4. Do the textbook exercises for that section. Add anything you got wrong to [[error-log]].

## End of each chapter
1. Answer **Test yourself** from memory, then check the sections in brackets.
2. Do the unit's **You should be able to** list closed-book, and tick only what you can really do.
3. Do the book's review questions (each unit note gives the page).
4. Finish the **Make** items, and set the unit's `status` to `studied`.

## Each week
- Read that week's bridges and explain each one aloud.
- Quiz yourself on [[history-timeline]] and [[formula-sheet]].
- Redo earlier error-log items after 1, 3 and 7 days.
