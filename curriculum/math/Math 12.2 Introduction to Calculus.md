**Must know:** derivative from first principles (gradient method); power, product, quotient and chain rules; derivative of eˣ; first/second-derivative tests; ∫xⁿ dx; area between curve and axis. The book barely touches derivatives of trig and log functions, so don't over-invest there.

- [ ] Use derivatives for tangents and rates
- [ ] Find increasing, decreasing and extreme points
- [ ] Find antiderivatives and definite integrals
